Nova R Play Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Nova R Play is a learning app. It shows free educational videos using YouTube API Services. It has no accounts, and you don't sign in.

What stays on your device

Your notes, study progress, history, search history, watch positions, favourites, followed channels, playlists and settings are saved only on your device. They aren't sent to us.

What goes to YouTube and Google

When you browse, search or play a video, your device connects directly to YouTube and Google. They receive what's needed to provide that, such as your IP address and what you request, and their own policies apply. The app uses YouTube API Services, and by using it you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. See also the Google Privacy Policy.

What we don't do

We don't collect, sell or share your personal data. The app has no analytics or advertising of its own.

Keeping information current

Video and channel details saved on your device are refreshed or removed within 30 days.

Removing your data

Clear your history and cached data in the app's Settings, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Children

The app doesn't knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children.
